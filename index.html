AMR Dodge – Privacy Policy

Atlas Entertainment · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Short version: AMR Dodge does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

What the game stores

To remember your progress, AMR Dodge saves the following information only on your device: your coins, best scores, level and XP, items bought in the in-game shop, daily rewards, missions, achievements, game statistics and settings (sound, music, vibration).

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not included in cloud backups.

What the game does not do

  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not use the internet. The app does not request the Internet permission.
  • It does not contain ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It does not offer purchases with real money.

Deleting your data

You can delete all game data at any time by uninstalling the app, or from Settings → Apps → AMR Dodge → Storage → Clear data on your phone. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing stored by us to delete.

Children

AMR Dodge does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
